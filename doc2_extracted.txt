1. Executive Summary
Agri Mart is a comprehensive, multi-module digital agricultural platform developed for GreenShield Industries Pvt Ltd. The platform integrates E-Commerce, AI-driven product recommendations, rental services, service booking, lead management, multi-warehouse operations, crop lifecycle management, equipment repair &amp; service management, and a finance incentive commission portal — all accessible through web and mobile applications.
The platform is designed to serve farmers, agricultural dealers, warehouse operators, procurement teams, sales teams, service centers, and administrators through role-based access, enabling end-to-end digitization of the agricultural supply chain.

2. Project Overview &amp; Objectives
The Agri Mart platform is designed as a unified digital ecosystem that connects every stakeholder in the agricultural value chain — from farmers purchasing products to warehouses fulfilling orders, procurement teams tracking crop cycles, sales teams managing leads, service centers repairing equipment, and administrators overseeing the entire operation.
2.1 Business Objectives
Enable farmers to discover, purchase, and receive agricultural products through an intelligent e-commerce platform.
Provide AI-driven product recommendations based on crop type, soil, acreage, and farming stage.
Facilitate rental of agricultural equipment on daily, weekly, or monthly basis.
Enable booking of agricultural services (tractor, spraying, harvesting, etc.) via GPS-based smart requests.
Manage dark warehouses with intelligent order allocation based on GPS and PIN code mapping.
Track farmers through complete crop lifecycle stages for timely product recommendations and follow-ups.
Manage post-sale repair and service of agricultural equipment across a 1,500+ dealer network.
Provide a finance incentive commission portal for tracking dealer and sales incentives.
Deliver native mobile experience on Android and iOS for customers and internal teams.
2.2 Technology Partners
This project is commissioned by GreenShield Industries Pvt Ltd, Bangalore, and will be executed by ROI Infotech Solutions. The project reference is Ref No: 2026-27/P163, dated 19-08-2026.

13. Multi-Warehouse Management System
A centralized, cloud-based platform for automated order management, warehouse allocation, procurement tracking, sales follow-up, and customer crop lifecycle management.
13.1 Intelligent Warehouse Assignment Engine
The system supports two assignment modes:
Option 1 — Automatic Assignment:
The system automatically assigns the order to the nearest dark warehouse based on GPS Coordinates, PIN Code, and Warehouse Radius. Flow: Customer Places Order → Payment Success → GPS Detection → Nearest Warehouse Search → Inventory Check → Auto Assignment → Warehouse Notification → Admin Notification → Procurement Portal → Sales Team Portal.
Option 2 — Manual Assignment:
Change Warehouse / Assign Another Warehouse
Split Order across multiple warehouses
Hold Assignment (pause for review)
Reassign Warehouse
Manual mode is useful during stock shortage, warehouse overload, or emergency situations.
13.2 End-to-End Order Workflow
Step 1: Customer Places Order
Step 2: Payment Success
Step 3: System Detects GPS / PIN Code
Step 4: Finds Nearest Warehouse with Available Stock
Step 5: Auto or Manual Warehouse Assignment
Step 6: Warehouse Accepts Order
Step 7: Packing
Step 8: Dispatch
Step 9: Delivery
Step 10: Procurement Follow-up Starts (Crop Stage Tracking)
Step 11: Sales Follow-up (Upselling &amp; Retention)
Step 12: Crop Completed → Customer Retention Campaign
