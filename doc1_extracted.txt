1. What is Agri Mart?
Agri Mart is an end-to-end digital agricultural platform commissioned by GreenShield Industries Pvt Ltd, Bangalore. It is not merely an e-commerce website — it is a comprehensive ecosystem that digitizes the entire agricultural value chain for the Indian farming community.
At its core, Agri Mart connects farmers with agricultural products, services, and expertise through an intelligent, AI-powered platform. But it goes far beyond a simple online store. It includes warehouse management, crop lifecycle tracking, equipment repair services, dealer commission management, and native mobile apps for every stakeholder in the ecosystem.
Think of it as a "one-stop digital platform for everything a farmer needs" — from buying seeds and fertilizers, to renting equipment, to getting their sprayer repaired, to receiving timely reminders about what to apply next in their crop cycle.
2. Who Are the Stakeholders?
Agri Mart serves multiple user types, each with their own portal or app:
2.1 Farmers / Customers
The primary end-users. They buy products, rent equipment, book services, and receive AI recommendations. They access the platform through the website or mobile app.
Access via: Customer Portal, Mobile App
2.2 Dealers / Retailers
Agri Mart works through 1,500+ dealers who sell agricultural equipment and products. Dealers manage their own service centers and earn commissions.
Access via: Dealer Portal, Service Center Portal
2.3 Warehouse Operators
Dark warehouses that store products and fulfill customer orders. Each warehouse receives orders automatically (or manually assigned) and manages dispatch.
Access via: Warehouse Management Portal, Warehouse Mobile App
2.4 Procurement Team
Tracks farmers through their complete crop lifecycle. Monitors what products each farmer has purchased, what stage their crop is at, and follows up with timely recommendations.
Access via: Procurement Portal, Procurement Mobile App
2.5 Sales Team
Manages leads, follows up with customers, upsells products, and tracks sales targets. Every customer interaction is logged in the CRM.
Access via: Sales Team Portal, Sales Mobile App
2.6 Service Centers
Post-sale service network that repairs agricultural equipment. Manages job cards, spare parts, estimates, and warranty claims.
Access via: Service Center Portal, Technician App
2.7 Administrators
Super admins and admins who oversee the entire platform — products, orders, users, reports, content, and system configuration.
Access via: Super Admin Portal, Admin Portal
2.8 Finance Team
Manages dealer incentives, commissions, and payouts through the Finance Incentive Commission Portal.
Access via: Commission Portal
3. What Does the Platform Do?
The Agri Mart platform is built around five major functional pillars:
Pillar 1: E-Commerce &amp; AI
The core shopping experience. Farmers browse products, get AI-powered recommendations based on their crop, soil, and farming stage, add items to cart, and checkout online. The AI engine asks questions like "What crop are you growing?" and "What's your soil type?" to suggest the right fertilizers, pesticides, and seeds.
Key Components: Product Catalog, AI Recommendations, Shopping Cart, Checkout, Order Management
Pillar 2: Warehouse &amp; Logistics
When a farmer places an order, the system automatically figures out which warehouse is closest to them (using GPS and PIN code), checks if the product is in stock, and assigns the order to that warehouse. The warehouse packs and dispatches it. The procurement team then starts tracking the farmer's crop to provide follow-up support.
Key Components: GPS-Based Warehouse Assignment, Inventory Management, Order Fulfillment, Delivery Tracking
Pillar 3: Rental &amp; Services
Farmers can rent equipment (weeders, cutters, sprayers) on daily/weekly/monthly basis. They can also book services like tractor hiring, harvesting, spraying, and soil testing. The system captures the farmer's GPS location and service requirements and automatically assigns the request to the right service provider.
Key Components: Equipment Rental Marketplace, Service Booking, GPS-Based Service Requests, Availability Calendar
Pillar 4: Crop Lifecycle Management
After a farmer buys products, the procurement team tracks them through 7 crop stages: Soil Preparation → Sowing → Manure/Fertilizers → Irrigation → Weed Protection → Harvesting → Storage. At each stage, the system reminds the procurement team to follow up and recommends the next set of products the farmer will need.
Key Components: 7-Stage Crop Tracker, Follow-Up Calendar, Product Usage Timeline, Automated Recommendations
Pillar 5: Service &amp; Commission
The Repair &amp; Service Management system handles post-sale equipment repairs across the 1,500+ dealer network — from job card creation to warranty claims. The Finance Incentive Commission Portal tracks dealer sales, calculates commissions, and manages incentive payouts.
Key Components: Job Card Management, Repair Tracking, Spare Parts Inventory, Commission Calculation, Payout Management
4. The Complete Farmer Journey
Here's how a typical farmer interacts with the Agri Mart ecosystem:
Step 1: Registration
The farmer downloads the Agri Mart app or visits the website. They sign up using their mobile number (OTP verification). They fill in their farm details — location (GPS), farm size, crops cultivated. This data goes into the CRM.
Technical Enablers: Mobile OTP Login, Farm Details, GPS Capture
Step 2: AI Consultation
The farmer clicks on the AI Recommendation Engine. They answer questions about their crop, soil type, acreage, current farming stage, pest problems, irrigation method, and budget. The AI engine then recommends specific fertilizers, pesticides, seeds, and equipment.
Technical Enablers: AI Questionnaire, Product Recommendations, Best-Sellers Display
Step 3: Shopping &amp; Checkout
The farmer browses the recommended products, adds items to cart, applies any coupon codes, and proceeds to checkout. The system validates delivery serviceability at their location (via GPS/PIN code) and shows the nearest warehouse that can fulfill the order.
Technical Enablers: Cart, Coupons, Delivery Validation, Payment Gateway
Step 4: Order Fulfillment
After successful payment, the system automatically assigns the order to the nearest warehouse. The warehouse receives the order, packs it, and dispatches it. The farmer receives SMS/Email/Push notifications at every step.
Technical Enablers: Auto Warehouse Assignment, Packing, Dispatch, Delivery Tracking, Notifications
Step 5: Crop Follow-Up
Once the order is delivered, the procurement team's portal shows this farmer as an active customer. Based on the crop cycle, the procurement team follows up at each stage — reminding the farmer about the next set of products they'll need, scheduling visits, and capturing product usage feedback.
Technical Enablers: Crop Stage Tracking, Follow-Up Calendar, Product Recommendations, Feedback Capture
Step 6: Rental &amp; Services
The farmer can rent equipment for a specific period or book services like tractor hiring or harvesting. The GPS system captures their farm location, and the request is sent to the admin for assignment to the nearest service provider.
Technical Enablers: Rental Booking, Service Request, GPS Location Capture, Provider Assignment
Step 7: Service &amp; Support
If the farmer's equipment needs repair, they book a service through the app. A job card is created, the equipment is picked up or dropped at the service center, repaired, and returned. The farmer tracks the entire process.
Technical Enablers: Job Card Creation, Repair Tracking, Delivery, Warranty Management

7. How the Modules Connect
One of the most powerful aspects of Agri Mart is how all modules work together as a single, unified system. Here's how data and workflows flow across modules:
Customer Registration → AI → CRM
When a farmer registers, their farm details (crop, soil, acreage, location) are captured. The AI engine uses this data for recommendations. The CRM captures the farmer as a lead for the sales team to follow up.
Technical Integration: Unified user profile database
Order Placement → Warehouse → Procurement → Sales
When a farmer places an order, it flows to the nearest warehouse for fulfillment. The procurement team sees the order and starts crop lifecycle tracking. The sales team sees the new customer for relationship management.
Technical Integration: Order event triggers across all portals
Product Purchase → Crop Stage → Next Recommendation
When a farmer buys fertilizer during the "Manure &amp; Fertilizers" stage, the procurement team records it. The system knows the next stage is "Irrigation" and pre-loads irrigation product recommendations for the next follow-up.
Technical Integration: Crop lifecycle data drives recommendations
Service Request → GPS → Assignment → Tracking
When a farmer books a service, GPS captures their location. The admin assigns the request to the nearest service provider. The farmer tracks progress in real-time.
Technical Integration: GPS and assignment engine integration
Equipment Repair → Job Card → Spare Parts → Billing
When equipment comes in for repair, a job card is created with complaint details. The technician diagnoses, estimates, and records parts used. The system bills and manages warranty claims.
Technical Integration: End-to-end service workflow
Sales → Commission → Payout
When a dealer sells products, the commission portal calculates their incentive based on targets, product categories, and volume. The finance team reviews and processes payouts.
Technical Integration: Sales data feeds commission engine

9. Key Challenges &amp; Considerations
Rural Connectivity &amp; Mobile Adoption
Many farmers may have limited internet connectivity. The platform must be optimized for low-bandwidth environments with progressive loading, offline capabilities, and lightweight mobile app design.
Mitigation: Optimized mobile experience, offline mode, SMS-based fallbacks
User Education
Farmers may need guidance on using digital platforms. The AI chatbot, simple UI, and regional language support (if needed) are critical for adoption.
Mitigation: AI chatbot, intuitive UX, multilingual support
Last-Mile Delivery
Delivering to rural addresses with accurate GPS and PIN code mapping is challenging. The PIN code serviceability API and multi-warehouse model address this.
Mitigation: GPS-based warehouse assignment, PIN code validation
Dealer Network Management
Managing 1,500+ dealers requires robust dealer onboarding, training, and performance tracking. The commission portal and dealer management system are key.
Mitigation: Dealer portal, commission tracking, leaderboard
Data Accuracy
Crop lifecycle tracking depends on accurate data entry by procurement teams. Training and simplified interfaces are essential.
Mitigation: Simplified procurement app, mandatory field validation
Seasonality
Agricultural activities are highly seasonal. The platform must handle peak loads during sowing and harvesting seasons.
Mitigation: Scalable VPS infrastructure, load balancing
Third-Party Dependency
Payment gateways, SMS providers, logistics partners, and AI APIs are external dependencies. Service level agreements and fallback mechanisms are needed.
Mitigation: Multi-provider support, fallback channels
Regulatory Compliance
Data protection laws, GST compliance, agricultural regulations, and app store policies must be adhered to.
Mitigation: GDPR/local data protection, GST integration, compliant app design
